Privacy Policy

The Simplest Metronome · Last updated: September 29, 2026

The Simplest Metronome does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

No accounts

The app has no sign-in.

No analytics or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and makes no network requests.

Settings stay on your devices

Your tempo, selected sound, appearance and haptics preference are saved on your device so the app remembers them. If you use the Apple Watch app, tempo and sound are sent directly between your own iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. This data never leaves your devices and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
