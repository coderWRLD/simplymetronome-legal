Frequently asked questions

How do I set the tempo by tapping?
Tap the large BPM number in rhythm. After two taps the tempo follows you. Pause for two seconds to start over.

How do I change the tempo on Apple Watch?
Turn the Digital Crown, use + and −, or tap the BPM number in rhythm.

I can't hear the click.
Check that your iPhone isn't muted and the volume is up. The app plays alongside music from other apps, so lower that audio if it covers the click.

Can I turn off the vibration?
Yes. Open Settings (the gear icon) and turn Haptics off. On Apple Watch, the setting is in the watch app's own settings.
